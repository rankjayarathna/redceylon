Tour Overview

A family-friendly week built around variety and manageable travel days. Family Sri Lanka opens on the coast at Negombo, moves through Sigiriya and Kandy's heritage sites, and finishes with relaxed beach time — a mix designed to hold the attention of both kids and parents.

Why You'll Love This Tour

  • Shorter driving days than RedCeylon's adult-paced signature tours
  • A mix of active sightseeing, culture and beach downtime for all ages
  • Comfort-tier hotels selected with family rooms and pools in mind
  • Village and ox-cart experiences that engage children directly

Tour Highlights

  • Negombo lagoon and fish market on arrival day
  • Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress (with rest stops en route)
  • Village tour by ox cart and traditional catamaran near Sigiriya
  • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and a Kandyan dance show in Kandy
  • Relaxed beach time on the west coast to close the trip

Route Map

Approx. total private-vehicle distance: ~600 km over the full circuit.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 — Arrival, Negombo

Arrive and transfer to Negombo for rest and recovery. Optional visit to the fish market and lagoon. Overnight Negombo.

Day 02 — Negombo to Sigiriya

Drive north to the Cultural Triangle, with a spice-garden stop en route. Overnight Sigiriya.

Day 03 — Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Village Experience

Morning climb of Sigiriya (paced for families). Afternoon village tour by ox cart and traditional catamaran with a home-cooked lunch. Overnight Sigiriya.

Day 04 — Sigiriya to Kandy

Drive south to Kandy via a spice garden. Afternoon at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic; evening (seasonal) Kandyan dance show. Overnight Kandy.

Day 05 — Kandy to Bentota, Beach

Drive to the west coast. Afternoon at leisure at your beach resort. Overnight Bentota.

Day 06 — Beach at Leisure

Free day for swimming, a river-safari boat ride, or simply relaxing by the pool or beach. Overnight Bentota.

Day 07 — Bentota to Colombo, Departure

Morning transfer to Colombo for onward flight. Tour ends.

Best Time to Travel

Recommended: Dec–Mar, Jul–Sep. A family-friendly route with a beach finish, so both windows below work well, especially outside the hottest midday hours for sightseeing days.

  • Dec–Mar — dry conditions across the Cultural Triangle and the west coast
  • Jul–Sep — also generally dry for this route
  • Plan Sigiriya and village activities for the morning to avoid the midday heat with children
  • Bentota's calmer waters suit families better than more exposed south-coast beaches
