Tour Overview

A themed journey built around locations traditionally associated with the Ramayana in Sri Lanka. Ramayana Trail moves from Colombo through Kandy and Nuwara Eliya's Sita Amman Temple to Ella and the south, connecting sites of spiritual and cultural significance along a scenic route.

Why You'll Love This Tour

  • A dedicated themed circuit rather than a generic cultural tour
  • Includes the Sita Amman Temple, one of the trail's most significant sites
  • Pairs spiritual sites with Sri Lanka's hill-country and southern scenery
  • Suitable for pilgrimage travellers and culturally curious visitors alike

Tour Highlights

  • Colombo sites associated with the Ramayana tradition
  • The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy
  • The Sita Amman Temple near Nuwara Eliya, a key Ramayana Trail site
  • Scenic hill country around Ella linked to the tradition's narrative
  • A closing visit to southern sites associated with the trail

Route Map

Approx. total private-vehicle distance: ~750 km over the full circuit.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 — Arrival, Colombo Ramayana Sites

Arrive and visit sites in and around Colombo associated with the Ramayana tradition. Overnight Colombo.

Day 02 — Colombo to Kandy

Drive to Kandy via a spice-garden stop. Afternoon at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. Overnight Kandy.

Day 03 — Kandy Sightseeing

Full day exploring Kandy's temples and Kandy Lake, with evening (seasonal) Kandyan dance show. Overnight Kandy.

Day 04 — Kandy to Nuwara Eliya, Sita Amman Temple

Drive into the hill country, visiting the Sita Amman Temple, one of the Ramayana Trail's most significant sites. Overnight Nuwara Eliya.

Day 05 — Nuwara Eliya to Ella

Scenic drive through tea country to Ella, with stops at sites linked to the trail's narrative. Overnight Ella.

Day 06 — Ella to South Coast

Drive to the southern coastal belt, visiting sites traditionally associated with the trail's concluding chapters. Overnight south coast.

Day 07 — Departure

Morning at leisure before transferring to Colombo for onward flight. Tour ends.

Best Time to Travel

Recommended: Dec–Mar, Jul–Sep. This themed route touches Kandy, the hill country and the south, so the windows below balance conditions across all three.

  • Dec–Mar — dry conditions across Kandy, the hill country and the south
  • Jul–Sep — good in the hill country; the south-west coast sees more rain
  • Nuwara Eliya is cooler year-round — bring a light layer for the Sita Amman Temple visit
  • RedCeylon can arrange a specialist guide with detailed knowledge of trail sites on request
